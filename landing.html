verified_user Regulatory Compliance

NIDANA complies with: GCP-Ayurveda (Ministry of Ayush), CDSCO New Drugs & Clinical Trials Rules 2019, ICMR-NIA Ethical Guidelines, ALCOA++ Data Integrity Standards, CTRI Registration Mandates, and IEC Approval Requirements.

security Data Security

  • Patient identifiers are pseudonymized/hashed on the blockchain (SHA-256)
  • End-to-end encryption for all data in transit and at rest
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) via Fabric MSP identities
  • Immutable audit logs for every regulated action (non-repudiation)
  • Hyperledger Fabric permissioned blockchain with Raft consensus

gavel Patient PII Protection

Patient Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is never stored in plaintext on the blockchain. Only hashed references are recorded on-chain; actual PII resides in a separate access-controlled, encrypted off-chain system.
